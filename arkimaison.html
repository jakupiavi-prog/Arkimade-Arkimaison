 JOURNAL / DESIGN NOTES

The feeling
behind a room.

A thoughtful interior is as much about how you feel as what you see.

A home changes throughout the day. Morning light stretches across stone. At noon, a deep overhang offers shade. By evening, the garden becomes part of the interior.

Designing with daylight means thinking beyond the size of a window. It is about orientation, depth, texture, and the way a room invites you to pause. A carefully placed opening can frame a tree, soften a threshold, or make an ordinary moment feel extraordinary.

At Arkimaison, this is where the conversation begins: not with what a house looks like, but with how it feels to live there.
